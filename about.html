About the project

A narrow AI company idea built around one measurable finance problem.

PLQNX is being repositioned around receivables operations for Indian manufacturing suppliers rather than a general-purpose AI assistant. The purpose is to help finance teams reduce overdue balances and manual collection work with verified data and controlled automation.

Who it is for

Manufacturing finance teams first.

The initial customer profile in the blueprint is a finance manager or owner at a manufacturing MSME with roughly ₹5–100 crore annual turnover and 50–500 B2B invoices per month, often using Tally or Zoho Books plus spreadsheets, email and WhatsApp.

15%+
Potential pain signal

One discovery trigger is more than 15% of receivables overdue, or one person spending 10+ hours a week on follow-up.

What we are not building

The boundaries are part of the product.

×

No payment rail

Customer collection money should not enter PLQNX-controlled accounts.

×

No lending or factoring

Financing receivables requires regulated partners or authorization and sits outside the first product.

×

No autonomous legal action

The software may organize evidence; qualified humans decide legal escalation.

×

No generic ERP

The first product stays focused on receivables workflow rather than becoming an all-purpose accounting suite.

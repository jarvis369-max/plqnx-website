ABOUT PLQNX · PRE-LAUNCH

Small beginnings.
An expansive vision.

PLQNX is an independent, pre-launch AI startup project. We're beginning with CORE, an invite-only assistant for learning, coding and thoughtful creation.

OUR ORIGIN

From the smallest
to the infinite.

Inspired by the Planck scale, our name represents the possibility of beginning with something small and building toward much bigger ideas. That is inspiration, not a claim that our technology operates at the Planck scale.

Our first priority is to make one useful product work well before expanding into new tools.

WHERE WE ARE TODAY

Real beta. Clear boundaries.

PLQNX CORE offers live conversational AI with saved account conversations, English and Telugu interaction, chat export and management tools.

Registration is invite-only and limited to five beta accounts. CORE is an early-stage project and its responses may be inaccurate.

The problem

Collections are often manual, fragmented and hard to prioritize.

The starting customer is a manufacturing supplier using tools such as Tally or Zoho Books plus Excel, email and WhatsApp. The product focuses on overdue receivables, follow-up work and unreliable cash visibility.

₹

Late payments

Ageing balances pile up while finance teams struggle to decide which account deserves attention first.

↻

Manual follow-up

Calls, emails, notes and spreadsheets make promise tracking inconsistent and time-consuming.

?

Weak visibility

Owners lack a trustworthy view of disputes, promises, expected cash and collection outcomes.

Core capabilities

One workflow from ledger data to approved action.

The product is deliberately narrow: upload or connect receivables data, reconcile it, rank what matters today, draft evidence-grounded actions, capture outcomes and measure value.

01

Portfolio

Total due, overdue ageing, disputed amount and expected cash in one finance view.

02

Prioritized accounts with a plain-language reason for every recommendation.

03

Customer ledger

Invoices, credits, payments, notes, promises and disputes in a traceable history.

04

Reminder drafts grounded in verified ledger facts, with human approval before sending.

05

Capture dates, amounts, reasons, owners and deadlines so nothing disappears in chat or spreadsheets.

06

Outcomes

Track overdue value influenced, DSO trend, promises kept, adoption, errors and time saved.

Daily user story

Built around the finance manager’s 9:00 a.m. routine.

The product should get a new finance user from uploaded data to an approved reminder quickly, while preserving evidence and control.

All FAQs ↗
What is PLQNX?

PLQNX is an independent, pre-launch AI project inspired by the Planck scale. Our working product is CORE, an invite-only assistant beta.

Can anyone use PLQNX CORE?

Anyone can explore this public website. CORE currently requires an invitation and account; registration has a five-account beta limit.

Is the beta free?

Invited users can currently access CORE without a subscription. The beta has usage limits and its future availability or pricing is not yet established.

Can CORE remember my conversations?

CORE saves conversations to your account. It uses a limited recent-chat context when generating responses; it does not have unlimited memory.

Is PLQNX an incorporated company?

Not yet. PLQNX is a proposed brand for an independent pre-launch AI startup project.

How is my information handled?

See the beta privacy notice. Avoid sharing passwords or sensitive information in chats.

OUR APPROACH

Powerful ideas start small.

We build one useful product first, learn directly from real users, and expand only when our technology demonstrates value. The Planck scale inspires our name, not a claim that our software operates at that physical scale.

Practical intelligence, thoughtfully built.

Our founding principles: useful software, transparent prototype status, human oversight, careful data handling and responsible scaling.

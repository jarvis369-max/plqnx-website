One focused product

PLQNX CORE is a receivables operating system.

No generic chat assistant, coding suite or creative AI catalog. The product is centered on one painful B2B finance workflow: getting invoices collected with better prioritization, evidence and control.

Six product surfaces

The version-one experience.

1

Onboarding & import

Company, users, ageing rules and CSV/Tally-style data upload with reconciliation.

2

Portfolio

Total due, ageing buckets, disputes and expected cash.

3

Daily queue

Prioritized accounts with transparent reasons and manual override.

4

Customer ledger

Invoices, credits, payments, notes, disputes and promises.

5

Action composer

Evidence-grounded reminders with approval states and auditability.

6

Outcomes

Recovered or influenced value, DSO trend, promises kept, errors and time saved.

Build now

Start with reliable accounting workflow, not flashy autonomy.

The immediate product scope is invoice import, ledger, ageing, queue, reminder drafts, promise tracking, audit log and basic roles. Integrations, forecast, partner dashboard and APIs come after proof.

✓

Deterministic totals

Ageing and accounting calculations use code and rules, not generative AI.

✓

Human-approved communications

Normal reminders require customer-employee approval before sending.

✓

Audit trail

Every action should be reconstructable from evidence, reviewer and outcome.

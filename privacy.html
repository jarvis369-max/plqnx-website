Privacy approach

Design the data flow for minimum necessary access.

This page reflects the privacy-by-design requirements in the product blueprint. It is not a substitute for a final legally reviewed privacy notice.

Pre-launch notice: production use should not begin until customer/controller roles, processor terms, security measures, retention and incident response are documented.

Data minimisation

Import receivables data needed for the workflow. Avoid unrelated bank narration, employee records or personal contacts.

Access controls

Use role-based permissions, MFA for administrators, tenant isolation and periodic access review.

Encryption and secrets

Use TLS in transit, managed encryption at rest and keep secrets outside source code.

Vendors

Cloud, AI, email, messaging, analytics and support providers should be recorded in a vendor register with confidentiality, deletion and incident obligations.

Retention and deletion

The product should support contract-end export, deletion workflows and deletion evidence, with retention aligned to applicable requirements.

Incidents

A named response team, evidence preservation, customer communication and applicable reporting clocks should exist before production use.

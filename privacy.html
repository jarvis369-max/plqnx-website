BETA PRIVACY NOTICE · PRE-LAUNCH

Your information.
Clear expectations.

This notice describes the current CORE private beta, not a general promise of production-grade data protection. Updated September 2026.

Beta notice: Do not submit passwords, payment details, medical records, government IDs or other sensitive information to the chat. PLQNX is an independent pre-launch project.

What we collect

To operate invited accounts, CORE stores a username, a salted derived password hash, hashed session tokens and session expiry information. It stores your conversation titles, chat messages and daily request totals. For abuse prevention, the backend uses hashed identifiers derived from incoming IP addresses for limited-rate windows instead of storing raw IP values in those rate-limit records.

How chats are processed

When you send a message, the backend retrieves a limited number of recent messages from the selected conversation and sends them with your new message to the configured Google Gemini AI service to produce a response. PLQNX does not claim its AI service processes data solely within this website or that conversations are end-to-end encrypted.

Where information is kept

CORE runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database for accounts and saved conversations. The browser keeps your login token in session storage. The homepage's optional live demo can also store a configured endpoint in local storage and a beta access code in session storage on that browser. Don't use shared devices for private beta access.

Retention and deletion

Login sessions expire after seven days unless signed out earlier. Conversation data remains in the database until deleted through the chat interface or removed during project maintenance. Deleting a conversation removes it from the project database, subject to any infrastructure backups and third-party processing policies. The beta does not yet include a self-service account-deletion or data-export-all-accounts flow.

Third-party providers

Cloudflare supports site backend and storage. Google Gemini provides AI responses. Their own policies and retention practices may also apply. Your conversations should not contain information that you would not want sent for external AI processing.

Cookies, storage and analytics

The beta uses browser session storage for authentication. The website does not currently advertise an account-linked behavioral analytics service. Infrastructure providers may process network and operational information when delivering the site.

Accuracy and safety

AI-generated answers can be wrong or incomplete. Don't rely on CORE for urgent medical, legal or financial decisions without qualified independent advice.

Privacy questions

A dedicated public privacy email address is not yet established. Existing invited testers can use the trusted invitation channel for privacy concerns. Avoid sending sensitive details through the feedback-report tool.

This is a descriptive early-beta notice that should be reviewed and updated before broader public registration or a commercial launch.

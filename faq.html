FAQ

What PLQNX CORE is — and what it is not.

What problem does CORE solve?

It is designed to help manufacturing finance teams prioritize overdue accounts, centralize the customer ledger, draft evidence-grounded reminders, track disputes and promises, and measure collection outcomes.

Is this a general AI chatbot?

No. The website and product direction now focus on receivables operations rather than open-ended chat, coding, writing or creative assistance.

What data does it need?

The MVP starts with invoice and payment records, typically through CSV/Tally-style exports. It should reconcile source totals before live use.

Does AI calculate invoice totals?

No. Ageing and accounting totals should use deterministic code and accounting rules. AI is reserved for tasks such as reply classification and reminder drafting under controls.

Can CORE send legal threats or recover money autonomously?

No. Legal escalation is decision support only, and legal/financial actions sit outside the MVP. The product should not lend, factor invoices, operate TReDS or move buyer-to-seller funds.

How is success measured?

Metrics include overdue value influenced, DSO change, promises kept, finance-user activity, fabricated-fact rate, renewal, gross margin, support load and time to first value.

The public site is a pre-launch demonstration. Pricing, market targets and milestones are planning hypotheses from the founder blueprint and still need real customer validation.

Pre-launch terms

Clear boundaries for a controlled receivables product.

PLQNX remains a pre-launch project. These website terms describe a demonstration and should receive qualified legal review before commercial use.

Purpose

CORE is intended as B2B receivables workflow software: ledger visibility, prioritization, reminders, promises, disputes and outcomes.

Human approval

Customer-facing reminders and higher-risk actions are designed around named human approval. The demonstration does not send real customer communications.

Excluded activities

The version-one product does not move customer funds, operate a TReDS platform, finance or purchase invoices, approve loans or autonomously issue legal demand notices.

Accuracy

Accounting totals and statuses must be reconciled to source data. AI-generated drafting can still be wrong and requires review before use.

Commercial status

Pilot prices, subscription ranges, funnel targets and growth milestones shown on this site come from a planning blueprint and are not validated promises.

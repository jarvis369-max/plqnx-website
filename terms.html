Pre-launch status: PLQNX is an independent project, not yet an incorporated business. These terms describe a limited experimental beta.

Access and eligibility

The live CORE beta is invitation-only, currently limited to five registered accounts, with up to 25 AI requests per user each day. You must use an invitation code provided to you with authorization and comply with the laws applicable to you. Additional access or account recovery is not currently guaranteed.

Account security

Keep your username, password and private beta invitation secure. Do not publish beta access codes or use another person's account. Sign out on devices you do not control.

Acceptable use

Use CORE for lawful learning, coding, idea development and other responsible purposes. Do not attempt to bypass rate limits, disrupt the service, attack accounts, or submit content that you do not have the right to share. Do not submit credentials or sensitive personal information.

Experimental AI

CORE generates responses using an external AI model. Output can be mistaken, incomplete or inappropriate for your needs. Verify important information before acting. CORE does not replace a qualified professional.

Content and availability

You remain responsible for what you submit and how you use generated output. This experimental service can be limited, changed or unavailable without advance notice. Future product concepts listed on our website are not current services or launch commitments.

Account and conversation controls

Use the chat interface to delete individual conversations and sign out of active sessions. A self-service account deletion or guaranteed recovery feature is not currently available. See the beta privacy notice for handling of account and chat data.

Brand status

PLQNX is a proposed project brand. Website content does not represent a claim of corporate registration, trademark registration, product certification, regulatory approval or a confirmed public-launch date.

Questions

A dedicated public legal/support channel is still being prepared. Invited users should contact the project through their existing trusted invitation channel.

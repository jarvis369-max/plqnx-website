Prove pain. Build narrowly. Scale only after evidence.

Stop, repair or scale

Product decisions follow evidence, not feature excitement.

■

Stop

If 30 qualified interviews do not produce at least three pilot commitments, change the segment, problem or offer.

↻

Repair

If totals are not trusted, fix ingestion and reconciliation before adding more AI.

↗

Scale

When renewals, weekly use and documented ROI repeat, scale the same segment and channel before expanding.
